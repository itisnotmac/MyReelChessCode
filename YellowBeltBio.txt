Yellow Belt
Age:29
ELO Score:1285
Background: Yellow Belt (Gifford Baughman), started playing chess at around 8 years old. His path he took walking home from school brought him through a popular park in his hometown of Minsk. This park has a section that is dedicated for people to go head to head in the game of their choice (with chess being the obvious favorite). There were always a couple dozen people sitting at the 2 person benches playing chess. A young Gifford was intrigued so he started walking a little slower so he could watch some of the games in real time. Eventually he would stop walking and stand next to a bench and watch 2 people in a concentrated battle of strategy. One day he asked one of the older gentlemen if they could teach him how to play. The old man didnt hesitate and told him to take a seat. Gifford was a chess natural picking up the game very quickly and securing his first win in under 4 weeks of playing. All of the old men said "beginner's luck"... but he proved them wrong as he systematically became a local chess phenom after securing a victory over every chess player in Minsk. He would later join some paid tournaments and earn a small amount of money by playing chess. He became locally famous because of his ruthless attack attack attack strategy. When Gifford was a teenager he was riding his bicycle to the convenience store near his home when he was struck by a vehicle from behind. He was in coma for almost a full year and when he came out of it, unfortunately, he no longer remembered how to play chess. He did learn the game again, but people who knew him before say he never really came back from that accident. His brain never really came back from the accident. The raw skill he used to defeat players with was gone forever. Truth be told, he didn't enjoy the game as much after the accident anyway. So there was no love lost on him there. Gifford Baughman still plays chess from time to time, still going by his youth nickname of "Yellow Belt".
